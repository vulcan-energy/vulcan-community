## Placement

- Interactive covered applications: preferably the supplied persistent plain-text
  **Powered by Vulcan** link, or an equivalent prominent **Vulcan
  Community**, **About**, **Legal** or **Source** item, containing this notice,
  the full applicable terms and the exact Corresponding Source link.
- Source distributions: `README`, `NOTICE`, package/crate/app metadata and the
  relevant source headers.
- Headless tools: `NOTICE`, documentation and package metadata, plus existing
  `--version` or About output where present.
- Apache-2.0 interoperability packages: their own source/package notices only;
  importing a thin codec does not by itself require an application-wide Vulcan notice.

Modified versions identify their modifications and replace the repository link
with their own exact Corresponding Source URL while preserving the origin and
non-affiliation fields. The notice and its navigation label may be translated,
restyled or reformatted without changing their substance or convenient prominence.
No Vulcan logo is required.
