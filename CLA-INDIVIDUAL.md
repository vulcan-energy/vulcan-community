Version 1.1, based on the Harmony Individual Contributor License Agreement 1.0

Thank you for your interest in contributing to Vulcan Community projects managed
by Home Energy Foundry Limited (**We** or **Us**). This contributor agreement
(**Agreement**) documents the rights granted by contributors to Us. To make this
Agreement effective, record your acceptance through a channel designated in
`CONTRIBUTING.md` — normally by posting the acceptance statement set out in the

so please read it carefully. The Agreement may cover more than one Vulcan
Community project managed by Us. This Agreement is a standing offer by Home
Energy Foundry Limited: it takes effect when Your acceptance is recorded, and no
countersignature is required.

## 1. Definitions

**You** means the individual who Submits a Contribution to Us.

**Contribution** means any work of authorship that is Submitted by You to Us in
which You own or assert ownership of the Copyright. If You do not own the Copyright
in the entire work, identify it under Section 3(d) before submission.

**Copyright** means all rights protecting works of authorship owned or controlled
by You, including copyright, moral and neighbouring rights, as appropriate, for
the full term of their existence including any extensions by You.

**Material** means the work of authorship made available by Us to third parties to
which the Contribution was Submitted. After You Submit the Contribution, it may
be included in the Material.

**Submit** means intentionally providing a specific work of authorship to Us for
inclusion in the Material through a pull request, a patch sent through the process
in `CONTRIBUTING.md`, or another channel We expressly designate in writing for
CLA-covered contributions. Issue reports, feature requests, comments, reviews,
design discussions and verbal communications are not Submissions unless You and
We expressly identify specific material in writing as a Contribution for
inclusion in the Material.

**Submission Date** means the date on which You Submit a Contribution to Us.

**Effective Date** means the date on which Your acceptance of this Agreement is
recorded through a channel designated in `CONTRIBUTING.md`.
